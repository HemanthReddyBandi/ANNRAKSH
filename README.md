# ANNRAKSH
🌾 ANNRAKSH – AI-Powered Food Wastage Prevention & Crop Planning System 🌾🤖🚜

About the Project

ANNRAKSH is an AI-driven agricultural management platform designed to prevent food wastage, reduce crop overproduction, and support sustainable farming. The system helps farmers make data-driven cultivation decisions by analyzing market demand, crop registrations, soil conditions, and regional agricultural trends.

By encouraging balanced crop production, ANNRAKSH aims to protect farmers from price crashes while ensuring stable food supply and market equilibrium.

Problem Statement

A major challenge in agriculture is that many farmers cultivate the same high-demand crops simultaneously. This often leads to:

Excess production and food wastage
Sudden market price crashes
Financial losses for farmers
Supply-demand imbalance

ANNRAKSH addresses these challenges through intelligent crop planning and AI-powered recommendations.

How It Works
Farmers register the crops they intend to cultivate.
The system tracks projected cultivation volumes.
When a crop reaches estimated market demand limits, the platform alerts new farmers.
AI suggests alternative profitable crops based on:
Soil type
Location
Climate conditions
Market demand
Farmers can make informed decisions, reducing wastage and increasing profitability.
